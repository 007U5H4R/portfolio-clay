# Card art provenance (TASK-202)

3D paper-cut art for the 13 `/projects` covers and the three home Featured Work cards. Generated with the Higgsfield connector, model `gpt_image_2_5`, quality `medium`, resolution `1k`, 0.5 credit per image (measured). Masters (PNG, 1344 px wide; 1024 px for the 3:2 RailCite layers) are kept outside the repo in `/Volumes/E Drive/Dev/.scratch/card-art/raw/`; `scripts/card-art/encode.mjs` turns them into the shipped files. No text appears in any art (prompted, and checked by eye).

Shared style line for covers: _Layered 3D paper-cut diorama, handmade cardstock and felt-textured paper layers with soft drop shadows between layers, light from the upper left, warm craft palette of cream, navy, terracotta, forest green and mustard, full-frame scene with a calm uncluttered upper band. No text, no letters, no logos, no people._

## Phone menu drawer art (TASK-255, Tushar 2026-10-18)

Generated with the Higgsfield connector, model `gpt_image_2_5`, quality `medium`, resolution `1k`; the model returned a baked checkerboard instead of alpha, so the shipped light masters were cut with Higgsfield `image_background_remover` and the dark landscape was keyed from flat white by `scripts/menu-art/encode.mjs` (which also erodes the alpha ~1 px and encodes transparent WebP, q80). Dark was generated with the light job as an image reference ("same composition, object placement and scale, re-coloured for dark mode"). Masters: `/Volumes/E Drive/Dev/.scratch/menu-origami/raw/masters/`. No text in any art.
