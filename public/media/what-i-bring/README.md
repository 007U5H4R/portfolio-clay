spend and the sha256 of every file: `/Volumes/E Drive/Dev/.scratch/what-i-bring/art/README.md` (the source of truth;

## TASK-203 scene parts (`scenes/<stage>/<light|dark>/<part>.webp`)

framing, IoU >= 0.99 against the light masters, so one set of masks serves both). Parts were cut top to bottom and each hole filled.
`bulb-lit` / `chip-lit` are brightened copies of their parts. Provenance, prompts, job ids and credit spend: `docs/reports/TASK-203.md`.
The old `<stage>/{back,mid,front}.webp` layers were removed (nothing references them); `landscape/strip.webp` stays.
