and **✗** when the asset fails the check.

The four checks:
- **metric**: no number, chart value, KPI or percentage that could read as a result
- **logo**: no real brand mark, wordmark or trademark
- **product UI**: no real or readable product interface (screens show sketches or nothing)
- **claim**: no text that states a fact about Tushar's work, employers or results
