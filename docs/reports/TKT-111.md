## Implementation

- `components/hero/Hero.tsx`: the POLAROIDS list was reordered and re-tilted, each with a centre tape. `sizes` went from 18vw to 11vw because the polaroids are now at most about 10.7 % of the canvas wide. The copy block was not touched (TKT-108's area).
- `app/globals.css` `.hero-polaroid*`: position, size, padding and tape are all in banner-canvas pixels. `--cpx: calc(100cqw / 3168)`, where `.hero-banner` is the ≥ 768 inline-size container from TKT-96 and the box is the whole canvas, so each photo tracks its paper exactly at every width ≥ 768. The old `max(136px, …)` floors are gone. The polaroids are still in the parallax image layer.
- **< 768:** still hidden (`MediaGate min={768}`), and no overflow at 390 (scrollWidth 390). The 4:3 crop cuts the large right sheet, and at 390 the polaroids would be about 30–45 px wide, so hiding them was the simpler of the two allowed options. At 390 the tall cream sheet and yellow note therefore show bare. If Tushar wants them filled on phones, a follow-up could show polaroids 1 and 2 there.

## Decisions

- **Corkboard left bare.** It has been clean since TKT-105 (coloured blank stickies on cork) and reads as intentional, with nothing garbled to cover. Moving all three polaroids to the wall also balances the scene. The partly cropped white papers at the far-left edge are fragments cut by the frame, not target canvases.
- **No new polaroid:** three papers and three polaroids (see above).
- **Postmark:** unchanged. At 768 its cancellation waves just touch the right polaroid's corner (multiply blend, which reads like a postmark). At ≥ 1024 it is clear of the polaroid.
