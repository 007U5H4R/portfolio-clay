# TKT-106 · Paper-over-paper parallax at every torn edge

Branch `m009/tkt-106` (from integration `18ece76`), worktree `Portfolio-m009-tkt-106`. Campfire TASK-103. Requested by Tushar on 2026-09-26: "for all paper cutout create a parallax effect in scrolling". He confirmed the design the same day ("the 'Feature Worked' should scroll above the hero section, it should be the case for all paper cut segment in all tabs"): a slow drift at about 0.5×, a 100–200 px overlap, not a pinned stack, included on phones, and zero under reduced motion. Recorded as Design.md §11 **Dev-96**.

## What changed

- **`app/globals.css`, new `/* TKT-106 */` block (CSS only, no component edits).** The rule is generic and keyed on the TornEdge contract (§3.1), where a torn section renders `[data-decor="torn"]` as its **first child**. Sibling tickets' new sections pick it up without anyone editing their components.
  - **Sheet (the torn section):** `:where(:has(> [data-decor="torn"]:first-child))` gets `position: relative; z-index: 1`. The rule has zero specificity, so existing z-indexes win (hero-sheet 4, case-study strip 2 and overview 3, band 1). One targeted rule, `.cs-article > .learned { z-index: 4 }`, keeps "What I learned" above the lagging overview (z 3).
  - **Lagging content (the section above the tear):** this is the sheet's previous sibling. For the band footer, whose previous sibling is `<main>`, it is `main`'s last child. It gets `animation: torn-lag` (`translate: 0 0 → 0 var(--torn-lag)`) on `animation-timeline: view(block)`, with `animation-range: entry-crossing calc(100% + 80px) entry-crossing calc(100% + 480px)`. Once its bottom (the tear) has risen 80 px into the viewport, the section above drifts at **0.5× page speed** from 0 to **200 px** over the next 400 px of scroll. This works the same way for any section height. The sheet keeps 1× and overlaps its lower ~200 px, and the lag then holds. Below 768 px the drift is 0.5× over 240 px (**120 px**).
  - **Why `entry-crossing` and not `exit`:** for a section shorter than the viewport, `exit 0%` only starts once its top reaches the viewport top. The short title blocks and `#about-cta` barely moved at a mid-viewport tear, which failed the `/about` band test (13 px). `entry-crossing 100%` is "bottom at viewport bottom" for every height. No gap opens above a drifting short section: it is itself a sheet whose predecessor started drifting earlier at the same rate and cap, so that predecessor's content fills behind it. After a scene opener, the strip that opens is plain page paper, which is what the title block already sits on (see the scratch review shots of `/work` and `/work/teachspark`). The **80 px start** (about the sticky header's height) keeps in-page anchors exact. A section parked under the header has not started drifting unless the next tear is more than 80 px into view, i.e. unless the section is shorter than about 747 px at 1440×900.
  - **Exclusions:** `main` itself (see the bug below) and `.hero-banner`, which keeps its TKT-96 image parallax. Scene openers are unaffected because their tear is their *last* child.
  - **Keyboard focus guard (WCAG 2.4.11):** a lagging section that `:has(:focus-visible)` drops the lag, so a focused link is never hidden under the next sheet. Pointer clicks don't trigger it.
  - The rules sit inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` and `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`. There are no JS listeners, and it works under Lenis because Lenis scrolls natively. It uses `translate` only, which composes with Reveal's `transform` and never shifts layout.
- **`Design.md` §11:** Dev-96 row, disposition "Tushar (2026-09-26)".

## Bug found and fixed during the build

The first generic selector also matched **`<main>`**, because the band footer is its next sibling. That translated the whole page body, which doubled the band overlap and made in-page anchor jumps land about 0.9 px low. `lenis.spec` "`#ask` lands under the header" failed with 74 vs 73. An A/B test in the same build with the animation disabled gave y 73.41 (off) vs 74.32 (on) at the same scrollY. The fix is `:not(main)`. A regression test now asserts `main` has `animation-name: none` and its last child has `torn-lag`.

## Tests

New `tests/e2e/torn-parallax.spec.ts`:
- `/` Featured Work tear and `/about` band tear at w1440. With the tear mid-viewport, the gap between the lagging section and the tear shrinks by ≥ 20 px (and ≤ 205 px) compared with the plain-scroll baseline (tear just below the fold), and `animation-name` is `torn-lag`.
- Reduced motion on both: `animation-name`/`translate` are `none`, no element runs `torn-lag`, and the sections scroll together (±1 px).
- `main` never lags; only its last child does. This is the scar for the bug above.
- A keyboard-focused hero CTA is the topmost element at its centre while the hero is fully lagged, and the hero's animation drops to `none`.
- No horizontal overflow on `/` and `/about` at 390 and 1440, checked every half viewport of scroll.

No existing test was edited or weakened.

## Notes / open

- **Pre-existing, not TKT-106:** `tests/unit/paper.test.tsx` fails 1 test ("fill=terracotta … snapshot") on base `18ece76`. 43d5f73 halved the band teeth without refreshing the snapshot; the refresh is `f220e3d`, which is not in this base. This ticket touches no TSX or snapshot.
- The overlap is by design (Dev-96): the bottom ~200 px of each section above a tear is covered once that tear is in the upper part of the viewport. On `/` that covers the hero CTAs mid-scroll, as Tushar asked. Keyboard focus undoes the lag, as described above.
- **Anchors:** an anchor that targets a lagging section at least ~747 px tall lands exactly (at 1440×900), including `#ask`. An anchor to a shorter section, or to an element near the end of a lagging section, can land up to 0.5 × (how far the next tear is past the 80 px start) lower. `regressions-m009` (ChapterNav / TKT-90b) and `lenis.spec` pass.
- **Short sections under a 200 px overlap:** on `/work` the 389 px title block has the lower half of its "Work" h1 under the index sheet at a mid-viewport tear. That's the ~200 px Tushar specified. If it reads as too much, the lag could be capped at a percentage of section height (`translate: 0 min(200px, 35%)`), but a short sheet followed by a taller one would then show a strip of bare page paper under the tear. I kept the uniform 200 px and am raising it for his eye rather than deciding it.
