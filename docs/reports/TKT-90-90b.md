| 3 | Chapter link click → hash updates, no scroll (TKT-83's intermittent w1024 failure) | Made deterministic: open the deep dive and click a chapter link in the same burst. w1024 + w1440 10/10 fail, with the target still 4469–4970 px down | `lenis.scrollTo` clamps to its cached `limit`. Lenis refreshes that limit only from a ResizeObserver with a **250 ms debounce**, and the debounce restarts on every resize. So while the deep dive is still growing the page (chapters mount, lazy images, fonts), the limit is the old short page's and the jump is clamped to where the page already is. The TKT-83 failure was the same thing, with lazy loads re-arming the debounce. | `lib/smooth-scroll.ts` `scrollToTarget`: call `lenis.resize()` before `scrollTo`. That re-measures the limit and also resyncs `animatedScroll` to `window.scrollY`, which replaces the earlier manual sync line. |

## Decisions (EXE-20 delegated)
- **Wrap, not scroll + affordance**, for bug 1. The brief allowed either. Wrapping shows every filter with no hidden content, and needs no fade mask or visible scrollbar. The cost: at 390 the row takes two lines (about 50 px taller). This supersedes TKT-80's "scroll with a peek < 768" (Deviation 3).
- **Bug 3 fix is in `lib/smooth-scroll.ts`, not `lib/focus.ts`.** The brief named `lib/focus.ts`, but the Lenis scroll path is `scrollToTarget` in `lib/smooth-scroll.ts`; `focus.ts` only holds the modal `lockBackground`. TKT-83 flagged the same mix-up. No other row owns `smooth-scroll.ts`.
- "01 is current at the top of the page" follows TKT-85 finding 5. The nav now always has exactly one current item after mount; before, it had none until the first chapter reached the band.
