instead of Systems write System Thinking). Remove all other texts. Make it clean."*

Thinking · A Better Tomorrow**. The master was edited in place, not regenerated: every non-text pixel stays where it was, so the
clip registration (scale 1.912, x 362, y 6) still holds.

## The clip mask

The clip frames come from `hero-desk`, which still has the text, and the clip mask covers parts of four edited regions: "The
journey", the caption, the laptop's left edge and the mug's right edge. With the old mask, the playing clip painted
"…rney. / …y." and a "B" back over the clean banner.

The video and poster are not edited. Instead, `public/media/illustrations/hero-clip-mask.png` got feathered holes over
those text areas, which removes the clip there and lets the clean banner show through. Pixels where the clip actually moves
(face, hand, pen, dog; frame-to-frame range > 45/255) are excluded from the holes. 17,196 of 875,520 alpha values changed.
Composited over the cleaned banner, the clip differs from it by more than 40/255 on 0 edited-region pixels, checked on every
6th frame of 60.
