## What changed (presentation only)

- **AskPanel** (`components/ai/AskPanel.tsx`, new `AskPanelDecor.tsx`): a torn-edge notebook sheet
  (a clip-path tear on the top and bottom edges, dotted paper, the punched holes and red margin kept, a steel paperclip), the "Ask AI" Caveat
  lettering on a note-yellow highlighter with sparkles, "Meet Tushky" with a steel underline, and an
  idle intro. The intro has the honesty line on a pink torn note with a curly arrow, the Tushky badge
  with the hand note "Ask anything about my journey!", the greeting card and a green "Suggested
  questions" tape with a bulb. The six panel prompts are tinted cards (pink/blue/green/lilac/yellow/rose),
  each with a round lucide icon and a →. The cards are 2-up when the drawer body is ≥ 380 px (the 480
  drawer at ≥ 1440) and 1-up otherwise. The input is now a rounded field with a sparkle and a torn navy
  "Ask →". Below it are the quick-action chips and a footer row (mountain and leaf doodles plus
  "+ Turn curiosity into conversations."). The answer, empty and error states sit on an ivory card.
- **Shared pieces**: `SuggestedPrompts` gets a panel-only `cardStyle` variant, and `AnswerView` gets
  `idleIntro` and `cardStyle` slots. The home inline notebook passes neither, so it renders exactly as before.
- **Behaviour unchanged**: the retrieval-only local provider, the five states, EVAL-012 (untouched),
  the native `<dialog>` focus trap, Esc/close/scrim, drawer vs bottom sheet (S21), and the prompt text and submit path.
  The honesty microcopy is still the shared `Microcopy` (S7).
- **CSS**: `/* TKT-104 … */ … /* end TKT-104 */` at the end of `app/globals.css`. The old TKT-77 panel
  body rules moved there. Everything uses paper tokens or `color-mix()` (tokens:check 13/13). All text
  is ≥ 14 px, and every Caveat note is `aria-hidden`. Targets are ≥ 44 px. The whole input field carries
  the rust focus ring. Reduced motion drops the card lift and adds no animation.

## Differences from the target that remain

- Tushky is the golden retriever, not the robot (Tushar's direction).
- The chip label is "Show my impact", not "Compare experiences" (Dev-49).
- The honesty note is set in Inter italic, not handwriting.
- In the 480 px drawer, card text wraps to 3–4 lines. The reference is drawn wider.
- On phones (< 768) the footer doodles and paperclip are hidden, and the quick chips are one
  swipeable row, so the cards keep their room.
- The collage sprite sheets were not used. The doodles are inline SVG.
