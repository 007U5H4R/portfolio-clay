## 3 · Shared question data

`tushky-questions.ts` is the only definition: `TUSHKY_SUGGESTIONS` `{label, query, category, entry}` and `CATEGORY_ICONS`. `ask-tushky-data.ts` re-exports both under the same names, so the drawer and its tests are unchanged apart from the data itself. The module does not import the knowledge index, so Home can import it without adding to `/` first-load JS: 160.1 kB gz against a 180 kB budget.

Deliberate change (Dev-66): the drawer now uses the Home spec's wording and order. "What AI products has he worked on?" replaces "What is his AI / cloud experience?" and still resolves as worded to `ai-products`. The AI icon is `CodeXml`, previously `Cpu`. The drawer agent's two remaps are kept:
- "Show me his product thinking process." sends the `discovery` prompt.
- "Walk me through a specific project." sends the `most-technical` prompt.

`tests/unit/ask-tushky-grounding.test.ts` pins every label to its entry, plus the shared identity. The pastels were strengthened to spec §13's hues (oklch mixes of paper tokens) and apply to both surfaces.

## 6 · Drawer launch behaviour

| Trigger | Result |
|---|---|
| Enter or send with text | `openPanel({ query, trigger: input })`. The drawer slides in from the right, the text becomes the user bubble and is submitted, and a sourced answer follows. The field clears. |
| Empty send | Opens the empty drawer |
| Card | `openPanel({ label, query, trigger: card })` asks that question |
| Hero "✦ Ask Tushky" CTA | Opens the empty drawer. `#ask` is the no-JS fallback. Modified clicks keep browser behaviour. |
| Close | Focus returns to the control that opened the drawer |
| Focusing the field | Does not open the drawer (Dev-64) |

## 7 · Responsive

- **≥ 1024:** two columns (43 / 57).
- **1024–1199:** cards go single-column inside the narrower notebook.
- **< 1024:** stacks in this order: title → lead → Tushky + sticky side by side → notebook.
- **768:** Tushky is 220 px and the cards are 2 × 3.
- **< 640:** cards are single-column, the composer is full width, and Tushky is 150 px.
- No horizontal overflow at 390 / 768 / 1024 / 1440. The section stays inline and never behaves like the drawer.

## 8 · Accessibility

- Cards are real `<button type=button>` elements with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. Every control is at least 44 px and shows the rust focus ring.
- The input's aria-label is "Ask Tushky anything about Tushar".
- The send button is labelled "Ask Tushky".
- The Caveat heading and subtitle have sr-only twins (EVAL-018 rule 5). Decorations are aria-hidden.
- Colour never works alone: every card has an icon and its text.
- The entrance runs only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and its transitions start only on reveal. The hidden state is applied only after hydration, so there is no JS-off hider. Focus entering the section reveals it.
- Reduced motion removes hover movement.

## 9 · Compromises

- **Dev-64:** the drawer does not open on focus. Opening a modal on focus is a WCAG 3.2.1 change of context.
- **Dev-65:** no paperclip at all, neither a button nor a decorative glyph. An inert glyph would still read as an attachment affordance. This matches the drawer's Dev-61.
- **Dev-66:** the drawer's copy, order, icon and pastels change to match Home.
- **Dev-67:** a new mascot generation (1.0 cr). The drawer's 231×280 head-and-shoulders image has no paws and is too small at 2x.
- **Dev-68:** the section keeps the site's 128 px transparent top rhythm (spec §19 asks for 80–110). The lead uses spec §4 copy, not the older line still visible in the reference image.
- **Dev-69:** the decoration count goes from 2 to 4 (the budget is 4). "Answers come from this portfolio's content…" and the inline answer states leave Home.
- **Versus the reference:** Tushky is a little smaller than in the image, and the paws rest on a CSS paper edge rather than a painted table.
