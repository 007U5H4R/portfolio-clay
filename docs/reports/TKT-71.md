## QA-010 fix (fix round 1 of 2, 2026-09-25) — nav fits at lg, no pill overlap at 1024

**Finding.** At 1024 the "Let's connect →" pill overlapped "Playground" by ~12 px on every route. Root cause, measured on the unfixed header (`/`, headless Chromium, no scrollbar): row inner width 896 px = brand 182.3 + 24 + nav (350.2 of Fraunces-18 text + 4 × 30.7 gap = 473) + 24 + actions 206.2 (pill 152.2 + 10 + Ask 44) → **0 px slack**. Headless hid it two ways: the grid's `auto` brand column shrank 13.6 px (the wordmark had `min-width: 0`, so the nowrap subline spilled under the nav) and there is no scrollbar; a real Chrome window loses ~15 px to the scrollbar at 1024, which is the ~12 px `Playground ∩ pill` overlap QA saw.

**Fix** (`app/globals.css`, TKT-71 banner; within Design.md §4.1 — same five items, same order, Fraunces 18, 44 px hit areas, pill and Ask unchanged): an **lg-only** (1024–1279) spacing scale — nav gap `clamp(18px, 3vw - 12px, 36px)` (18.7 px at 1024 → 26.4 px at 1279) and row gap 16 px; from 1280 the §4.1 `clamp(18px, 3vw, 36px)` / 24 px values apply unchanged. `.header-wordmark` loses `min-width: 0` so the brand column can never be squeezed below its subline again (if the row ever runs out of room the pill overflows the header and the test fails, instead of copy silently spilling).

**Measured after the fix** (`/`, headless): Playground → pill gap **41.2 px at 1024** (≈ 26 px with a 15 px scrollbar), **94.6 px at 1280**, **154.6 px at 1440**; brand → Home the same by symmetry (the nav column centres); right edge slack 0 (the actions cluster sits flush to the gutter, as designed).

**Scar** — `tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts` "header controls never overlap or overflow at 1024 / 1280 / 1440 (QA-010)" (w1024 project; w1440 project natively and resized to 1280 via `setViewportSize`), on `/`, `/work`, `/about`: exactly 8 visible controls (brand + 5 nav + pill + Ask); no pair of bounding boxes intersects; none leaves the header; no control's **text** overflows its own box (text-range union vs box — catches the brand squeeze while ignoring the ink underline's designed 2 px bleed); adjacent controls ≥ 16 px apart outside the pill+Ask cluster (the 16 px margin stands in for the scrollbar headless lacks). **Run against the unfixed build first: 3/3 failed** — 1024 reported `Playground ∩ Let's connect → = 11.9px` (on `/about`, whose brand squeeze differed) plus the squeezed brand; 1280/1440 failed only on the brand-squeeze/min-gap clauses before the cluster exclusion was added. After the fix: 3/3 pass.
